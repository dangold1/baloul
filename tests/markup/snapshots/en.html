Who we are

More than twenty years of supporting clients.

BALOUL is an agency for finance, pensions and insurance based in Holon. Our professional work began in 2003, and in 2014 it grew into an agency that supports clients across all of these areas together.

BALOUL began in 2003 with Yitzhak Baloul, an insurance agent with more than two decades of experience. Yitzhak came from the high-tech world and holds a degree in electrical and electronics engineering and an MBA.

In 2014 the practice grew into a broader, more established agency. Today we support clients in finance, pensions and insurance, with an emphasis on personal service, transparency and long-term thinking.

FAQ

Questions that come up in almost every call.

Short answers in clear language. Questions about pensions, insurance, savings and planning are on each service's page.

How do I start getting my finances in order?

Start by gathering your income, commitments, savings, assets and goals. Once the picture is clear, you can set priorities and decide what needs to change and what is right to leave as it is.

What should I prepare for the first call?

Almost nothing. In the first call it is enough to tell us about your situation and what is on your mind. If you have annual statements from your pension fund or a list of your insurance policies at hand, that helps, but we can gather most of the information together later.

How much does it cost?

The introductory call and the initial review are free and carry no obligation. As an agency, we are paid by the institutional bodies and insurance companies, as the law sets out, and we detail this on our disclosure page. If a particular case calls for a paid service, we say so up front.

Where do we meet?

Our office is at 15 Ehud Manor Street in Holon. You can meet us at the office, and much of the day-to-day work is also done by phone and WhatsApp, whichever suits you.
